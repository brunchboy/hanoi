Can The Recursive Algorithm Work?

  • It may seem like we’re cheating here: recursive algorithms can feel like that until you prove to yourself they work.
  • In programming, the very idea of recursion (or even the simpler notion of “re-entrant” code, which could be in use more than once at the same time even if it was not itself recursive) was once controversial, and difficult to implement.
  • You have to keep track of all the variables that a recursive function uses in a way that keeps each simultaneously-running copy from interfering with the others, and remember where you’ve reached in each instance for when you return back to it.
  • Now, essentially all languages (and processor architectures) support it, through the use of call stacks and stack-allocated variables.
  • Of course, if you don’t get your base case or algorithm right, you can run out of (overflow) your stack space, hence the popular programming help site, stackoverflow.com.
