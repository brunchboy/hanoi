Towers of Hanoi: Recursive Step

If the tower we are trying to move is more than one ring tall (say num-rings), we can

  • (recursively) move all but the biggest ring out of the way, then
  • move that ring to the target post, and
  • move the smaller tower on top of it:

(hanoi (dec num-rings) from (other-post from to))
(move from to)
(hanoi (dec num-rings) (other-post from to) to)
