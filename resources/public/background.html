Towers of Hanoi: But Why?

  • I first encountered this puzzle as a child, I think our family had a small six-ring version of it.
  • I was able to solve for a few rings, but by four or so, I’d lose track in the middle.
  • I never learned a general solution, and didn’t find it that interesting yet.
  • In high school AP computer science, we learned the recursive solution and implemented it in Pascal. This got me interested.
  • I studied the move lists generated by my program as solutions, and thought there must be a pattern there.
  • Eventually, I figured it out! But my teacher didn’t believe me!
  • When we used it again to teach recursion as TAs for Introduction to Algorighmic Programming, I had interesting things to say about it.
  • Let’s retrace some of that journey today.
